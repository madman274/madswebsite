[Maddy Weavers](https://www.instagram.com/maddyweavers/) is a freelance editor and writer about architecture. She is deputy editor of Real Review (2023–) and edited for Apartamento (2021–23) and Drawing Matter (2020–21). She studied architecture at the University of Bath and now lives in London. Her activity includes essays, interviews, copywriting and editing, commissioning, editorial direction and strategic direction. Her email is maddylweavers@gmail.com.
