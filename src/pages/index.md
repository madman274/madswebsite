[Maddy Weavers](https://www.instagram.com/maddyweavers/) is an editor and writer for architecture, art and design publications and organisations. She is deputy editor of [Real Review](https://real-review.org), having previously edited for [Apartamento](https://www.apartamentomagazine.com/archive/madelon-vriesendorp/) and [Drawing Matter](https://drawingmatter.org). She is currently working with [Guggenheim Bilbao](https://www.guggenheim-bilbao.eus/en/the-collection) and is a frequent collaborator of [Bofill Taller de Arquitectura](https://www.bofill.com/journal/whats-old-is-new-again/); she is editing the first monograph dedicated to a building by Bofill. She studied architecture and has written for publications including [AA Files](https://bookshop.aaschool.ac.uk/?product=aa-files-83), [MacGuffin](https://www.macguffinmagazine.com/issues/macguffin-the-wall), and [The Architectural Review](https://www.architectural-review.com). For enquiries please contact maddylweavers@gmail.com.
