[Maddy Weavers](https://www.instagram.com/maddyweavers/) is a freelance editor and writer about architecture. She is deputy editor of [Real Review](https://real-review.org) and has edited [Apartamento](https://www.apartamentomagazine.com) (2021-23) and [Drawing Matter](https://drawingmatter.org) (2020-21). She studied architecture in Bath and now lives in London. Her activities include essays, interviews, copywriting and editing, commissioning, editorial direction and strategic direction. Her email is maddylweavers@gmail.com. 

Publications: [AA Files](https://bookshop.aaschool.ac.uk/?product=aa-files-83), [Apartamento](https://www.apartamentomagazine.com/archive/madelon-vriesendorp/), [El Croquis](https://elcroquis.es/en/products/236-6a), [The Architectural Review](https://www.architectural-review.com), [Real Review](https://real-review.org), [MacGuffin](https://www.macguffinmagazine.com/issues/)...
