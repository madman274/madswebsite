[Maddy Weavers](https://www.instagram.com/maddyweavers/) is a freelance editor and writer about architecture. She is deputy editor of Real Review (2023–) and edited for Apartamento (2021–23) and Drawing Matter (2020–21). She studied architecture at the University of Bath and now lives in London. Her activities span essays, interviews, copywriting and editing, commissioning, editorial direction and strategic direction. She has worked with 6a architects, AA Files, The Architectural Review, Bofill Taller de Arquitectura, El Croquis, Guggenheim Bilbao, Leonard Koren, MacGuffin, Madelon Vriesendorp, Michael Marriott, Openhouse and Richard Wentworth among others.
Her email is maddylweavers@gmail.com.
